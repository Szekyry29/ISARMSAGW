Easy Self-Enrollment

Students can now enroll themselves in subjects without waiting for the registrar. Simply:

  • Select academic year
  • Choose regular or irregular status
  • Pick their section
  • Enroll in available subjects

Smart Filtering

The system automatically filters subjects based on:

  • Student year level
  • Regular vs Irregular status
  • Section assignment
  • Semester selection

Teacher Assignment

Registrar can assign teachers to section subjects:

  • View all enrollments
  • Assign teachers per subject
  • See enrollment counts
  • Override drops if needed

Enrollment Status

Students can see their enrollment status with:

  • Enrolled subjects highlighted
  • Teacher assignments visible
  • Easy drop/withdrawal option
  • Bulk enroll capability

Regular & Irregular

System differentiates between:

  • Regular: Year-level restricted subjects
  • Irregular: Any subject in program
  • Automatic filtering by type
  • Clear status indication

Current Enrollments

Students can manage current enrollments:

  • View all active enrollments
  • See teacher assignments
  • Drop subjects anytime
  • Check sections & semesters
